What are cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make sites work, remember preferences, and provide basic analytics information to site owners.

How we use cookies

CasuallyAI is a content site with no advertising, no e-commerce, and no user accounts. Our cookie usage is minimal by design:

  • Strictly necessary cookies: some basic session functionality may require cookies to operate correctly. These are essential and cannot be disabled without breaking site functionality.
  • Analytics cookies: if we use an analytics tool (such as Plausible Analytics), it may set cookies to help us understand aggregate traffic patterns — for example, which articles are most read. Any analytics we use is chosen specifically to avoid collecting personally identifiable information.

We do not use advertising cookies, social media tracking cookies, or any third-party marketing cookies.

What we don't do

We do not use cookies to build profiles of individual users, serve targeted advertising, or share data with data brokers or marketing networks.

Managing cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to refuse cookies entirely or delete them after each session. Note that disabling all cookies may affect the functionality of some websites, though it should have minimal impact on CasuallyAI given our limited cookie usage.

For guidance on managing cookies in your specific browser, visit aboutcookies.org.

Changes to this policy

If our cookie usage changes materially, we will update this page. The current version is always available here, identified by the "last updated" date above.
