Who we are

CasuallyAI (accessible at CasuallyAI.co) is an independent research and publishing project based in England. For any privacy-related queries, contact us at contact@CasuallyAI.co.

What data we collect

We collect minimal data. Specifically:

  • Newsletter subscriptions: if you subscribe to our mailing list, we collect your email address. This is used solely to send you new experiment notifications. We do not sell or share it with third parties.
  • Contact emails: if you email us directly, we retain that correspondence for the purpose of responding to you.
  • Analytics: we may use privacy-respecting analytics (such as Plausible or similar) to understand aggregate traffic patterns. No personally identifiable information is collected through analytics.

What we do not collect

We do not use advertising trackers, third-party marketing pixels, or behavioural profiling tools. We do not collect payment information — this site is free to read.

How we use your data

Email addresses collected via the newsletter signup are used only to send you updates about new experiments published on this site. You can unsubscribe at any time via the link included in every email.

Data storage and security

Email addresses are stored with our newsletter provider. We take reasonable steps to ensure data is held securely, but no method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of any personal data we hold about you. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at contact@CasuallyAI.co.

Third-party links

This site may contain links to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites and encourage you to review their policies independently.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Changes will be reflected by the "last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the site constitutes acceptance of the current policy.
